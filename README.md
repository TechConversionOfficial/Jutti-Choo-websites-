## About the business

- **What it does:** Jutti Choo sells handcrafted Punjabi footwear with embroidered, embellished, festive and everyday designs. The website presents named styles, product photographs, size options and care instructions.
- **Who it serves:** people shopping for bridal footwear, occasion wear, traditional outfits and everyday juttis, in India and international destinations served by its published shipping arrangements.
- **Brand story:** the official [About page](https://juttichooindia.com/pages/about) describes Sushant Sood's role in developing the online brand and credits artisans and craftspeople for its designs. These are statements from the brand itself.
- **Why the digital experience matters:** shoppers need to examine detailed embroidery, compare styles, check their shoe size and understand delivery and exchange terms before placing an order.

## The website challenge

Handcrafted footwear is a visually led purchase, but appearance alone is not enough to decide. A shopper comparing bridal and everyday juttis needs clear collections, close-up product imagery, correct size options, delivery information and practical care instructions. The brand's traditional craft identity also needs to remain recognisable in a modern online storefront.

This describes the **commerce and communication challenge** addressed by the publicly visible website. No independent evaluation of an earlier website, or documented pre-redesign defect list, was available for this record.

## How the website presents the solution

1. **Lead with the collection rather than technical detail.** Product photography and occasion-led categories communicate the range quickly.
2. **Separate important shopping journeys.** [Bridal Juttis](https://juttichooindia.com/collections/bridal-juttis), [Casual Wear](https://juttichooindia.com/collections/leather-juttis) and [Daily Wear](https://juttichooindia.com/collections/printed-juttis) are distinct routes in the store navigation.
3. **Let visitors explore individual styles.** Listings and product pages identify named designs, prices, photos and relevant size choices.
4. **Make sizing a visible part of shopping.** Product pages publish a conversion chart covering several regional shoe-size systems.
5. **Explain the purchase after the product.** Product pages include shipping, exchange, packaging, care and international-order information. Policies and current availability should be checked directly before purchase.
6. **Support discovery beyond the catalogue.** The homepage includes featured products, a journal and customer-review presentation, while About and Contact pages give a route to the business.
7. **Connect heritage with modern merchandising.** The saved project design concepts explore warm ivory backgrounds, burgundy accents, embroidered footwear photography, product categories and craft-led storytelling.

These are observations from the public website and saved presentation files, **not a verified internal development log**. The project artwork contains suggested interfaces that may not appear on the production store.

## Standards relevant to a fashion ecommerce website

These are **review references**, not claims of completed audits or certified compliance:

- [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals): test loading, responsiveness and visual stability on mobile and desktop.
- [WCAG 2.2, Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): evaluate contrast, product-image alternatives, keyboard access, size selectors and forms.
- [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials): review useful collection and product content, crawlability and search presentation.
- [Schema.org](https://schema.org/): assess accurate product, offer, organisation and breadcrumb markup where implemented.
- [India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework](https://www.meity.gov.in/data-protection-framework): consider how the store manages customer and enquiry information where applicable.

There is no independent report in this repository establishing Core Web Vitals scores, accessibility conformance, checkout-security certification or the results of a privacy audit.
